01

How rockets work

Rockets push hot gas backward to produce thrust. A launch vehicle carries a payload through the atmosphere and accelerates it until it reaches the required trajectory.

02

From launch to orbit

Many rockets use multiple stages. Empty stages are discarded so the remaining vehicle can continue accelerating. Reaching orbit requires enough horizontal speed as well as altitude.

03

What spacecraft do

Spacecraft may carry people, landers, rovers, telescopes, or scientific instruments. Their power, communication, navigation, and thermal systems help them operate far from Earth.
