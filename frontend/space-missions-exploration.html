01

Why explore space?

Space missions help scientists investigate planetary history, the Sun, Earth’s changing environment, and the conditions needed for life. Each mission is designed around specific questions.

02

Robots as explorers

Orbiters map worlds from above, landers study surfaces, and rovers travel across terrain. Cameras, spectrometers, drills, and other instruments collect evidence for scientists.

03

Planning a mission

Teams choose a destination, design the spacecraft, test systems, plan communications, and consider risks. Long travel times and limited power or bandwidth make careful planning essential.
