ASTRAQUEST WIRING CHALLENGE

Open index.html and follow the mission flow to Launch Sequence, then click START LAUNCH SEQUENCE.
The wiring challenge includes instructions, interactive wire matching, connection review, and a systems activation screen.
Wire connections can be made by dragging a source socket to a target socket or by clicking a source and then a target.
Five systems must be matched correctly: Engine Control, Navigation, Power System, Life Support, and Communication.
