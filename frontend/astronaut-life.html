01

Training for a mission

Astronauts practise operating spacecraft, using tools, responding to emergencies, and working as a team. Training prepares crews for routine tasks and unexpected situations.

02

Everyday life in orbit

Crew members schedule experiments, maintenance, meals, exercise, sleep, and communication with mission control. Microgravity changes how people move, eat, and handle everyday objects.

03

Protecting the human body

Exercise equipment helps astronauts maintain strength and bone health. Life-support systems manage breathable air, temperature, water, and other conditions needed for crew safety.
