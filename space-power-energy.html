01

Turning sunlight into electricity

Solar cells convert sunlight into electrical energy. Large arrays provide power to many spacecraft and orbiting laboratories.

02

When sunlight is unavailable

Spacecraft may pass into shadow or operate far from the Sun. Batteries and carefully planned power use help systems continue operating when solar generation is limited.

03

Managing spacecraft power

Power systems regulate voltage, monitor battery charge, and prioritize critical equipment. Engineers must balance available generation against the energy needed by each system.
