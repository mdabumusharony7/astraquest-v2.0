01

Why space food is different

In microgravity, crumbs and free-floating liquids can become hazards around equipment. Many foods are designed to limit crumbs and are packaged so they can be handled securely.

02

Planning astronaut meals

Mission planners balance calories, nutrients, variety, shelf life, storage volume, and crew preferences. Food must remain safe and appealing throughout the mission.

03

Preparing a meal in space

Some foods are ready to eat; others need water added or warming. Crew members use dedicated equipment and follow procedures to keep the galley clean and safe.
